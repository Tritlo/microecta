# Future work
+ Count equality-constrained edges, the piece `fromECTA` leaves out: a constraint correlates an edge's children, so its count is the size of an intersection rather than a product. No published result covers counting for tree automata with equality constraints, and the ground is not encouraging: emptiness is undecidable for the general class and EXPTIME-complete for sibling constraints ([Bogaert and Tison, STACS 1992](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-55210-3_181)), and counting the distinct terms of an ambiguous *unconstrained* automaton is already #P-hard, being #NFA ([Antonopoulos, Geerts, Martens and Neven, *Generating, Sampling and Counting Subclasses of Regular Tree Languages*, Theory Comput. Syst. 52, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00224-012-9428-x); [Arenas, Croquevielle, Jayaram and Riveros, *#NFA admits an FPRAS*, JACM 68(6), 2021](https://doi.org/10.1145/3477045) for the approximation). The joins this package builds count exactly because of their restricted shape — one constraint per argument, over an unambiguous product — not because counting constrained edges is tractable in general. So the realistic target is the term-search automaton's own structure, not a general algorithm. Sampling well-typed terms straight out of it is the payoff.
+ Retain one ECTA term per recursive member, so `groupBy`, `match`, `pmf`, and `countBy` can cross the recursive boundary in both layers. The term shape is already determined by the support the recursive builders construct; what is missing is carrying it beside the value without paying for it on the sampling path.
+ Term-level shrink rewrites validated by nodeRepresents against the support: propose subtree replacements from the support's own edges and let the automaton check acceptance, complementing the delivered size-ordered minimal search.
